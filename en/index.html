Osimo · Marche · Italy

A family table in the Marche countryside

Welcome to Agriturismo Giuggioloni, a farm restaurant in Osimo, in the province of Ancona. Our family brings the farm’s produce and the flavours of seasonal Marche cooking to the table.

View the English menuBook your table

From father to sons

Our story is a family story, passed from father to sons. Maurizio is our cook and prepares the food; Massimo manages the dining room and welcomes our guests. We grow what the seasons allow and use the harvest in our menu.

Two dining rooms, throughout the year

The indoor dining room has a long row of windows overlooking the garden. Our second room is outdoors and covered: its sides stay open in summer and can be closed in winter, so it can be used in the colder months too.

A garden for everyone

Children have a large green space to play in the restaurant’s garden, surrounded by nature, while parents can enjoy an aperitivo outdoors. Pets are welcome.

Parking and access

There is a large car park in front of the restaurant. The premises are wheelchair accessible.

A working farm

Our farm raises geese, hens, chickens, ducks, rabbits, lambs, goats, sheep, calves and pigs. It is a working farm, not an educational farm, and is not open for visits.

Opening times and reservations
